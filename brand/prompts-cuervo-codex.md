# El cuervo de Hemispheric — hoja de prompts para Codex (varias imágenes)

> Mascota elegida: **el cuervo que guarda y recuerda** (memoria local). Genera **cada bloque por separado** en Codex
> (uno = una imagen) y pide 3-4 variaciones de cada uno. Todas comparten el mismo ESTILO para que la familia sea
> coherente. Fondo **transparente** (o `#0A0A0A`). Sin texto salvo donde se indique.

## Estilo común (va en TODOS los prompts)
`Style: data-art / point-cloud — built from fine points and thin connecting lines, like a constellation; near-black
#0A0A0A or transparent background; a single amber #B45309 glow as the accent (eye / synapse / stored node); flat with
a subtle glow, clean and geometric; clever and warm, not cute; modern tech brand mascot; crisp, reducible to a small
size.`

---

## 1 · Mascota principal (perfil 3/4, posada)
`A stylized crow/raven mascot built from fine glowing points and thin lines (data-art), perched in a 3/4 view, calm
and clever, one eye glowing amber #B45309 like a synapse. Near-black #0A0A0A background. Modern tech brand mascot,
flat with subtle glow, geometric, no text.`

## 2 · Favicon / glifo (mínimo, una línea)
`A minimal single-line icon of a crow/raven head facing left, one amber #B45309 dot for the eye, made of a few clean
geometric strokes, high contrast, reducible to 16px, works in dark and light, transparent background, no text.`

## 3 · El momento clave: guarda un recuerdo
`A geometric data-art crow holding a small glowing amber #B45309 node in its beak (a stored memory), about to hide it,
built from points and thin lines, near-black #0A0A0A background, warm and clever, modern tech mascot, subtle glow,
no text.`

## 4 · En vuelo (para movimiento / hero)
`A stylized data-art crow in flight, wings spread, a trail of small glowing amber #B45309 points following it (memories
it carries), built from points and fine lines, near-black #0A0A0A background, dynamic but elegant, modern tech brand,
no text.`

## 5 · Logo lockup (con el nombre)
`Brand lockup: a small geometric data-art crow mark next to the word "Hemispheric" in an elegant serif (Instrument
Serif) in off-white, with a small amber #B45309 dot after the word, on a near-black #0A0A0A background. Balanced,
premium, minimal tech brand.`

## 6 · Spots para las docs (set de 3, mismo cuervo, acciones distintas)
`Three small matching spot illustrations of the same geometric data-art crow: (a) perching on a branch made of
connected nodes, (b) tilting its head looking at a glowing amber node, (c) tucking a node under a wing. Points-and-lines
style, amber #B45309 accent, near-black #0A0A0A background, consistent character, no text.`

---

## Cómo usarlas
- Genera 1-6 por separado; pide **varias variaciones** de la 1 y la 2 (son las que más se reutilizan).
- Mándame los PNG que te gusten y los **aplico**: favicon (2), Nav (5 o 2), loader (1 o 5), hero (1 o 4), docs (6).
- Si alguna sale muy recargada, añade `simple, few points, lots of negative space` al final del prompt.
