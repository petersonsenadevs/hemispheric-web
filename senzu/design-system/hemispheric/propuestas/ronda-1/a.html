Temporal reasoning (cat2) — where it counts

Engine Temporal (cat2) vs mem0
Hemispheric 16.2% +206%
mem0 5.3% —

Hemispheric stores facts with date and temporal context. mem0 doesn't. That's why it almost doubles mem0 on temporal recall — and why "when" matters more than "what" for long-running agents.
